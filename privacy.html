1. Who runs this site

Effect Lab (yilana.cn) is an independent, hand-built gallery of browser visual effects. Every effect on the site is written in plain JavaScript, Canvas 2D and CSS — there is no framework, no account system and no server-side database. This policy explains what data is involved when you browse the site and what choices you have.

2. What we do not collect

To be explicit, because most privacy policies are not:

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login. We never ask for a name, email address or password.
  • There is no file upload, no comment box and no contact form that stores data on a server.
  • There is no newsletter and we do not build marketing profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to anyone.
  • The effects run entirely inside your browser; nothing you draw, click or type into a demo is transmitted anywhere.

3. Server logs

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages records standard request data: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring URL, and your browser's user-agent string. This is used only to serve pages correctly, diagnose errors and prevent abuse. Logs are retained for a short period by our hosting provider and are not combined with any other information to identify individual visitors.

The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in operating a secure, working website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Local storage on your device

The site uses your browser's localStorage — not cookies — for two strictly functional values. These never leave your device and are not readable by other websites:

You can delete them at any time through your browser's site-data settings; the site will keep working, it will simply forget your preferences.

6. How to opt out of personalised ads

  • Decline advertising cookies in the banner on this site, or reopen it with the Cookie settings button at the bottom of this page. Declining still shows ads, but they are non-personalised.
  • Google Ads Settings — opt out of personalised advertising by Google.
  • aboutads.info (US) and Your Online Choices (EU) — opt out with participating vendors.
  • Network Advertising Initiative — further industry opt-outs.
  • You can also block all third-party cookies in your browser settings. The effects on this site will continue to work normally.

For more information about how Google uses data on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

7. Your consent choices

Consent is collected and stored by Google's own consent management platform, delivered through AdSense Privacy & Messaging. It is on Google's list of certified CMPs, and it records your choice through the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2) as well as Google Consent Mode v2. This site does not run a second, competing banner — two banners asking the same question would be both confusing and non-compliant.

Consent Mode defaults are set per region. Visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland start with advertising storage denied — ads are non-personalised until they make a choice in the Google message. Elsewhere advertising storage starts granted, so personalised ads may be served without a banner, and you can opt out at any time using the button below or Google Ads Settings.

You may change or withdraw your choice at any time — it is as easy as giving it:

The Google message is shown to visitors covered by European regulations (EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). If no Google message can be served in those regions — for example because it has not been published yet for this site, or an ad blocker prevented the script from loading — a lightweight fallback banner is shown instead, so that no advertising cookie is ever set there without a recorded choice. Outside those regions the fallback banner stays out of the way.

8. Third-party demo code

Part of the gallery showcases demos originally written by other developers, kept intact under the /repo/ path and displayed inside an embedded frame. Those demos remain the work of their respective authors, and attribution links back to the original source are included in the code where available.

Some of these embedded demos load JavaScript libraries from public CDNs (for example cdnjs.cloudflare.com or cdn.staticfile.org). When such a demo runs, your browser requests those files directly from the CDN, which will see your IP address and user-agent as part of serving the request. We do not control those providers; please refer to their own privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate data;
  • Erase data (the "right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

In practice we hold almost no personal data: the only information tied to you is stored in your own browser and can be cleared by you directly. If you have made a request and want it actioned on the server side, use the contact address below and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising when you have declined advertising cookies, and we do not knowingly collect information from consumers under 16.

China mainland users: the site processes only the technical data described above and does not transfer personal information across borders beyond ordinary website request handling.

10. Children

The site is a general-audience technical showcase and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes — for example if analytics or a comment system is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

1. 网站运营方

特效实验室（yilana.cn）是一个个人独立维护的浏览器视觉特效展厅。站内所有特效均使用原生 JavaScript、Canvas 2D 与 CSS 编写，没有使用框架，没有账号系统，也没有服务端数据库。本政策说明你浏览本站时会涉及哪些数据，以及你拥有哪些选择。

2. 我们不收集什么

大部分隐私政策写得很含糊，这里说得明确一些：

  • 没有账号、没有注册、没有登录，从不索取姓名、邮箱或密码。
  • 没有上传、没有评论区、没有会存数据的联系表单。
  • 没有邮件订阅，也不会建立营销画像。
  • 不出售、不出租、不交换任何个人数据。
  • 所有特效完全在你的浏览器内运行，你在演示区里的点击、绘制、输入不会被传送到任何服务器。

3. 服务器日志

与所有网站一样，提供这些页面的服务器会记录标准请求信息：IP 地址、请求时间、请求的页面、来源地址（Referer）以及浏览器的 User-Agent。这些信息仅用于正确返回页面、排查错误和防止滥用，由托管服务商短期保留，不会与其他信息结合用于识别个人。

该处理的合法性基础是运营一个安全可用网站的正当利益（GDPR 第 6(1)(f) 条）。

4. 保存在你设备上的本地存储

本站使用浏览器的 localStorage（不是 Cookie）保存两个纯粹功能性的值。它们不会离开你的设备，其他网站也无法读取：

键名用途保存时长
yilana.lang记住你选择的是中文还是英文，避免每翻一页就重置。直到你清除站点数据
yilana.consent记住你接受还是拒绝广告 Cookie，以免反复弹出提示。直到你清除站点数据

由于这两项是为实现你主动请求的功能（语言选择）以及履行你的同意决定所必需，因此不需要事先征得同意。你可以随时在浏览器设置中删除它们，网站仍可正常使用，只是会忘记你的偏好。

5. 广告 Cookie

本站通过 Google AdSense 获得收入支持。在你同意启用广告 Cookie 后，Google 及其合作伙伴可能设置 Cookie，用于展示广告、限制同一广告的展示频次、衡量广告效果；在你同意个性化广告的情况下，还会根据你此前访问本站及其他网站的记录来选择广告。

Cookie 名称与有效期由 Google 控制，可能发生变化；上表反映的是撰写时的常见取值。

6. 如何关闭个性化广告

  • 在站点横幅中选择「仅必要」拒绝广告 Cookie，或用本页底部的 Cookie 设置 按钮重新打开横幅。拒绝后仍会看到广告，但属于非个性化广告。
  • Google 广告设置——关闭 Google 的个性化广告。
  • aboutads.info（美国）与 Your Online Choices（欧盟）——对参与该机制的供应商统一关闭。
  • Network Advertising Initiative——更多行业性退订入口。
  • 你也可以在浏览器设置中直接屏蔽全部第三方 Cookie，本站特效不受影响。

关于 Google 如何在合作方站点使用数据，请参阅 Google 的相关说明。

7. 你的同意选择

同意由 Google 自家的同意管理平台（CMP）收集与保存，通过 AdSense「隐私权和消息」下发。它在 Google 的认证 CMP 名单内，会同时通过 IAB 透明度与同意框架（TCF v2）和 Google Consent Mode v2 记录你的选择。本站不再运行第二个与之竞争的横幅——两个问同一件事的弹窗既让访客困惑，也不符合规范。

Consent Mode 的默认值按地区设置。欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士的访客，广告存储初始为 denied——在 Google 消息中做出选择之前只投放非个性化广告；其他地区初始为 granted，即无需弹窗即可投放个性化广告，你仍可随时通过下方按钮或 Google 广告设置关闭。

你可以随时更改或撤回选择——撤回和给予同样容易：

Google 的消息面向受欧洲法规约束的地区（欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士）的访客。如果这些地区无法下发 Google 消息——例如本站尚未发布该消息，或广告拦截插件阻止了脚本加载——则改由轻量兜底横幅接管，确保在那里不会在没有记录选择的情况下设置任何广告 Cookie；其他地区不会出现这个兜底横幅。

8. 第三方演示代码

展厅中有一部分演示由其他开发者原始编写，以原样保留在 /repo/ 路径下并通过内嵌框架展示。这些演示的著作权归各自作者所有，代码中保留了可用的原作者链接标注。

其中部分演示会从公共 CDN（例如 cdnjs.cloudflare.com、cdn.staticfile.org）加载 JavaScript 库。当这类演示运行时，你的浏览器会直接向该 CDN 请求文件，CDN 因此会获知你的 IP 地址与 User-Agent。我们无法控制这些服务商，请参阅其各自的隐私政策。
