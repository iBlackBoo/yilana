Nebula Swirl 星空漩涡

Eighty particles in polar form, orbiting at different speeds — the ellipse comes from scaling y by 0.6. 八十个粒子用极坐标描述，各自以不同角速度公转——椭圆感是把 y 压到 0.6 换来的。

How it works原理拆解

Each particle stores only an angle a and a radius r. Every frame a increases by its own speed s, and the position is projected with cos(a)*r for x and sin(a)*r*0.6 for y. Squashing the y axis is what turns a flat circle into a tilted galaxy. 每个粒子只存角度 a 和半径 r。每帧 a 按自己的速度自增，位置用 cos(a)*r 算 x、sin(a)*r*0.6 算 y。把 y 轴压扁，正圆就变成了有倾角的星系。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Change 0.6 to 0.25 for an edge-on view, or 1 for a face-on spiral.
  • 2Give r a slow oscillation so the arms breathe instead of staying rigid.
  • 1把 0.6 改成 0.25 是从侧面看，改成 1 就是正面俯视的漩涡。
  • 2让 r 缓慢来回变化，旋臂会有呼吸感，而不是硬邦邦地转。
