Laser Grid 激光网格

Horizontal lines that get closer together as they approach the horizon — a fake 3D floor made of one loop. 横线越靠近地平线越密——一个循环就够了，是假 3D 地面。

How it works原理拆解

Each line's depth z cycles 0–800 via modulo, and the projected position uses 300/(z+1): as z grows the value collapses toward zero, squeezing lines against the horizon. The vertical fan is drawn separately with fixed angles, so only the horizontal rungs actually move. 每条线的深度 z 用取模在 0–800 之间循环，投影位置用 300/(z+1)：z 越大结果越趋近 0，横线就被挤向地平线。纵向的扇形是另画一遍、角度固定的，所以真正在动的只有横向的踏板。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Change 300 to 800 for a flatter, more distant horizon.
  • 2Add glow on every eighth rung to suggest a lit runway.
  • 1300 改成 800，地平线会更远、更平。
  • 2每第八条横线加发光，就像一条点亮的跑道。
