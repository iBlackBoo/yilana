Color Smoke 彩色烟雾

Puffs that grow as they rise and are drawn at 15% opacity, so dozens of them build up into smoke. 一团团会边升边变大、而且只用 15% 不透明度画的烟，几十个叠起来才成雾。

How it works原理拆解

Three things happen per frame: y decreases, r increases by 0.4, and x drifts by sin(y/20). The growing radius is what makes it read as expansion rather than translation. Because each puff is only 15% opaque, no single circle is visible — only the accumulation is. 每帧做三件事：y 减小、r 加 0.4、x 按 sin(y/20) 漂移。半径不断变大，是它看起来像「扩散」而不是「平移」的关键。因为每一团只有 15% 不透明，单看任何一个圆都看不出来——雾感来自累积。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Slow the radius growth to 0.15 for tighter, more defined plumes.
  • 2Randomise the hue range down to one colour family for tinted stage smoke.
  • 1半径增速降到 0.15，烟柱会更紧、形状更明确。
  • 2把色相范围收窄到同一个色系，就是舞台上的染色烟。
