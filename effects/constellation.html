Constellation 星座连线

Fifty nodes, and every pair closer than 90px gets a line whose opacity is the distance itself. 五十个节点，距离小于 90px 的两两之间连一条线，透明度直接用距离算。

Canvas 2DPairwise distanceAlpha by proximity
Live demo实时演示

Runs live on this page. Move the pointer, click, or drag — whichever the effect listens for. 就在本页实时运行。移动鼠标、点击或拖拽——看它响应哪一种。

How it works原理拆解

Two nested loops over the node array give the O(n²) pair pass — fine at 50 nodes, visibly slow at 500. Line alpha is 1 - d/90, so lines fade in continuously as nodes approach rather than popping at a threshold. The mouse is tracked but unused in the base version, which is the obvious hook for interactivity. 两层嵌套循环做 O(n²) 配对——50 个节点没问题，500 个就会肉眼可见地卡。线的不透明度是 1 - d/90，所以线是随距离连续淡入的，而不是踩到一个阈值突然出现。基础版里鼠标坐标取了但没用，这正是加交互的现成接口。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Link each node to the mouse as an extra edge for a stronger sense of touch.
  • 2Raise the threshold to 140px and reduce the node count to keep the line count sane.
  • 1把鼠标也当成一个节点连进去，触碰感会强很多。
  • 2阈值提到 140px 时记得降节点数，否则线会糊成一片。
