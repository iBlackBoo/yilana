Neon Canvas 霓虹画板

Hold and drag to paint glowing strokes; the whole neon look is one shadowBlur setting. 按住拖动就能画发光笔迹，整个霓虹感只靠一个 shadowBlur。

How it works原理拆解

A boolean drag flag is set on mousedown and cleared on mouseup, and mousemove returns early when it is false. Each stroke point is a filled circle with shadowBlur 22 and shadowColor set to the same hsl as the fill — that is what produces the halo. Because nothing clears the canvas, strokes accumulate like real paint. mousedown 置一个 drag 标志，mouseup 清掉，mousemove 里先判断标志再决定要不要画。每个点是一个实心圆，shadowBlur 设 22、shadowColor 设成和填充同一个 hsl——光晕就是这么来的。因为全程不清屏，笔迹会像真颜料一样叠加。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Raise shadowBlur to 40 for a soft tube light, drop it to 4 for a laser.
  • 2Use lineTo between consecutive points for a continuous stroke instead of a dotted one.
  • 1shadowBlur 调到 40 是柔光灯管，调到 4 是激光笔。
  • 2相邻点之间用 lineTo 连起来，就从点状变成连续笔迹。
