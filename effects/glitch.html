Glitch Text 故障文字

Two copies of the text stacked on the original, each shaking on its own offset keyframe. 文字叠了两层副本，各自跑一份错位的抖动关键帧。

CSSpseudo-elementsattr()
Live demo实时演示

Runs live on this page. Move the pointer, click, or drag — whichever the effect listens for. 就在本页实时运行。移动鼠标、点击或拖拽——看它响应哪一种。

How it works原理拆解

::before and ::after both pull their content from attr(data-t), so the text exists once in markup but renders three times. One copy is pink and runs the shake animation forward, the other is teal and runs it in reverse — the mismatch between them is what reads as a signal fault. ::before 和 ::after 都用 attr(data-t) 取内容，所以文案在 HTML 里只写一次、却渲染三遍。一层粉色正向播抖动，一层青色反向播——两层错位的那一点，就是「信号故障」的来源。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Raise the translate values from 3px to 8px for a much more violent glitch.
  • 2Add a clip-path band to one copy so only a horizontal slice jitters, like a dropped scanline.
  • 1位移从 3px 加到 8px，故障感会猛烈得多。
  • 2给其中一层加 clip-path 只留一条横带，就只有一条扫描线在抖，更像掉帧。
