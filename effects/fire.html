Fire Simulation 火焰模拟

Flames are just circles that shrink their hue as they age — the fire colours are a formula, not a palette. 火焰其实就是一堆会变色的圆——颜色是算出来的，不是配好的色板。

How it works原理拆解

Particles are emitted upward with negative vy and a slight random x drift; every frame vy increases, so they arc. The colour is hsl(20 + l*30, 100%, 50 + l*30), which means a fresh particle is bright yellow and a dying one is deep orange-red. The 0.2-alpha clear leaves a soft glow behind each flame. 粒子以负 vy 向上喷出，x 方向带一点随机漂移，每帧 vy 增加所以走抛物线。颜色是 hsl(20 + l*30, 100%, 50 + l*30)：刚生成时是亮黄，快熄灭时是深橙红。每帧 0.2 透明度的清屏给火苗留了一圈柔光。

What to change可以改的地方

  • 1Add a tiny random vy per particle so the flame flickers instead of pulsing.
  • 2Emit from a wider base (×120 instead of ×40) for a bonfire rather than a candle.
  • 1给每个粒子一点随机 vy，火焰会跳动而不是整体脉动。
  • 2发射口从 ×40 放宽到 ×120，就从烛火变成篝火。
